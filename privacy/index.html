Smalta Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

In short

Smalta collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Everything it does happens on your Mac. The App Store version doesn’t even have network access. The developer runs no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

What Smalta reads

  • To show what takes up space, Smalta reads the names, sizes and dates of the files and folders you give it access to.
  • To find duplicates, it compares the contents of files in the folders you choose.
  • To find apps you no longer use, it reads when each app was last opened. To keep the caches of open apps safe, it checks which apps are running.

This information never leaves your Mac and is kept only in memory while a scan runs.

What Smalta stores

Only on this Mac and only for itself, in its own app folder:

  • its settings;
  • permissions for the folders you opened for it;
  • a short scan history: the date, how much junk was found and how much free space the disk had.

To remove all of it, delete Smalta and the folder ~/Library/Containers/ua.smalta.Smalta.

Your personal files

Smalta never deletes Photos, Music, Mail or Messages and never offers them for deletion. It deletes only what you selected, and by default moves it to the Trash.

Notifications

If you turn on scheduled auto-clean and allow notifications, Smalta tells you how much space it freed. Notifications are created on your Mac itself.

Purchases

Smalta Pro and the free trial are bought through the App Store and handled by Apple. Smalta only learns whether you have Smalta Pro or an active trial — never your payment details. How Apple handles purchases is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

What Smalta does not do

  • it has no sign-up and no accounts;
  • it contains no analytics, ads or third-party libraries;
  • it does not send data to the developer or to third parties, and no data is sold;
  • it does not track you across other apps and websites.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you have allowed sharing analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymized crash and usage statistics. Smalta itself collects no such statistics.

Correspondence

When you write to the support address, I see your email address and whatever you send, and I use it only to reply.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page and in the app with a new date.
